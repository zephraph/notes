The conceptual notion that repeated stimulus like reminders can build internal pressure which leads to a negative association of said stimulus. 

An example may be a reminder to catch up with someone. After repeated failures to act on said reminder, the idea of catching up with said individual may become less appealing over time solely due to the failure to act. That negative feeling becomes tied to the thought of the individual which further inhibits action.

I hypothesize that people with [[ADHD]] experiences this phenomena to a higher degree given our challenge with executive function and focus.
