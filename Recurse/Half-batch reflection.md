## Reflecting on my experience so far

I'd came into [[Recurse|RC]] with three areas to help govern my time:

1. [[Recurse#Principles to govern my time at RC]]
	- I started with a principle of `Completion is never the goal`, but I decided to drop that mid way through the first half of my batch. It was a bit of a defense mechanism that I erected because I feel like I struggle with finishing projects that I start myself. I didn't want this time to be clouded with guilt. The interesting learning here is that despite the principle, the guilt still formed. I felt like I didn't do enough, didn't stay focused enough, didn't have enough to show.
	- Overall while I still aspire to these principles, they were more aspirational than they were a mechanism to govern my time. Given that, I'm not sure how helpful they've been overall. A *principle* should be ingrained, but creating a principle doesn't mean that it *is* ingrained. There's a chasm between creation and enactment that requires internalization and accountability.
