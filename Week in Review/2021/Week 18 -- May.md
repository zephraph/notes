Hey friends, back again. [[Week 17 -- May|Last week]] I mentioned I had some big news. If you haven't heard already, I'm leaving [[Artsy]]. This coming up Friday is my last day.

The last two weeks have been a lot of emotional processing. It's been hard in a lot of ways. This has probably been the best job I've ever had. I'm definitely incredibly fortunate to have worked with a few of my heroes. That said, I know it's time. As it goes with these things, when you really start considering leaving it's already past time for you to go. 

The last year has had it's ups and downs, but psychologically it was hard. I had to take time off at the beginning of the year for burnout. The emotional scarring that that's left has made continuing business as usual difficult at best. I'm really ready for a break.

## A reflection on the week

Work has been a bit of a whirlwind. I had 9 or 10 1-on-1s and I've got about that many this week. I conducted a few interviews which I found a little challenging. I owe the candidates honesty (which I gave) but I have to be careful not to color the interview with my own bias. I hope I did okay.
