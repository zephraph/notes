#idea 

Journaling is easy to forget and can be hard to build a habit around. If your therapist asks you to rate your anxiety level every day, it could be easy to skip or be lax. In the long term that doesn't help you.

What if you didn't have to remember. 

## An example flow

_You receive a text_

>Hi `<name>`! Is now still a good time for your `<frequency>` check-in?

_You respond with yes_

_Your phone rings_

>Hey `<name>`, good `<time of day>`. Let's get started with your check-in. On a scale of 0 to 10 how would you describe your anxiety today?

_More questions are asked and responses given. The call ends._

_You receive a notification after the transcript has been processed_
