- A concept used  at [[Artsy]] to defined the overview of a technical project. 
- Includes sections such as
    - **Motivation**
    - **Impact**
        - Systems and teams impacted. How it affects the business. What benefits will it have
    - **Approaches**
        - The general outline(s) for what the path of implementation(s) will be. 
    - **Risks**
        - Major obstacles that the project may face 
    - **Questions/Decisions**
        - Questions that need to be answered or decisions that should be made before proceeding with work
    - **Outcome**
        - Dedicated to describing what success looks like
    - **Context**
        - Historical context that may be relevant to  understanding the way things  currently work, links to examples, explanations of workflows, etc. An appendix of sorts
    - **Stakeholders**
        - Whose opinions do we want to make sure we hear and take into consideration before making any final decisions?
