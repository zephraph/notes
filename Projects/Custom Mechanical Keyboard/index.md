## Goal
*2021-05-24*

I want to build a custom keyboard. By _build_ I mean design and CNC mill the case and keycaps, design the PCB, choose the hardware components, and write the necessary firmware. 

The goal in and of itself isn't particularly unique. I've been particularly inspired by [[Kevin Lynagh]]'s [Notes from a year of building keyboards](https://kevinlynagh.com/keyboards/).
