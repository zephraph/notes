I've long held a fondness for computers. They're a medium of such potential. I feel that mainstream computers, be it desktop or smartphone, have fallen short of their potential though. I believe this is largely due to the incentive structures that exist around these platforms. Smartphones in particular have largely become multi-media distribution platforms where corporations with well oiled product organizations vie for our attention... and they're very good at capturing it. 

We have an insidious relationship with our phones. They provide unparalleled access to information and utilities that we've come to depend on. They also are filled with addicting experiences that consume our time and leave us with anxiety.

We can do better. We should do better. 

In the course of these notes I'm going to outline some thoughts about how we could build a better computing experience. I'm mostly going to focus on the mobile use-case as I feel that's the area with the greatest need for change.
