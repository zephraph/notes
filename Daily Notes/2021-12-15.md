#game-idea

## Dungeon Core Game

A crowd sourced dungeon crawler where all the elements of the game are designed by players.

### Playable Roles

- Ancients -- Admins/moderators who can ban players, reject created assets, etc. This role is assignable only and cannot be selected by players.
- Deity -- A special classification for a game designer. Deities have the ability to create pocket worlds, design assets, design races, etc
- Dungeon Core -- A special entity that's capable of designing dungeons that mortals can enter to collect loot, gain XP, etc. Dungeon cores are corporal and can be killed. Any mortal who kills a dungeon core incurs a lasting negative debuff that marks them as core killers to other dungeon cores and makes the experience they gain after they kill a core to be harvestable. If a core-killer occupies a mortal territory every mortal within that territory killed by a core defender awards extra bonuses. 
- Mortal -- One of potentially many playable races that can experience the world created by deities and traverse through dungeons designed by dungeon cores
