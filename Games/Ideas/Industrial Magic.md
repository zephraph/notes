#idea #game

Industrial crafting game like factorio but entirely based on magic systems. 

Inspired heavily by runescape

Uses norse style runes in drawing system to create new spells. Spells are drawn on a radial tablet with each marker adding a modifier to the spell. The image below gives an example of what a rune may look like. 

![[Runes with ring example.png]]

Markings are added along a base line with a maximum of 8 segments (or 3 lines bisecting a circle). 

Modifier markings are placed along the lines 

Base runes can be placed in a circle around talismans
