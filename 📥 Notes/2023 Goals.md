## Highlights and commentary

> **don't rush to the “leap” of the infinite game, you must first walk solidly, and make sure that your basic needs are fully met**.

## Core Needs

1.  **Certainty/comfort:** You have food to eat and shelters to keep you warm and safe. You feel mentally comfort. You have a certain level of control over your life and know what to expect next.
2.  **Uncertainty/diversity:** If life is too comfortable and stable, we want to seek change. This is common to all human beings.
3.  **Importance:** We want to be unique, different, and feel needed. Many finite game is about creating the illusion of being important/unique. For example, studying for a Ph.D., becoming an influencer on social media, getting tattoos or sometimes even exaggerating one's own problems, aka "selling miserable", is also a need for importance.
4.  **Love and connection:** We all need friends, lovers, relatives, and need to experience a sense of connection and intimacy.
5.  **Growth:** We need to assure ourselves that we are "moving upward". This is why the end of the year and the beginning of the year are so important. This is an important time to look back on the year and experience whether "growth" happened.
6.  **Contribution:** We need to connect with society and others through **unconditional giving**. When something good happens in your life, what do you want to do? Your first instinct is to wish there was someone to share your joy with right? This is a very high level form of joy when it is shared with others and when you see the positive impact on others.
