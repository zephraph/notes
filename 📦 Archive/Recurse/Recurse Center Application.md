## Background

### Describe your programming background in a few sentences.

I really didn't start programming until college. I picked computer engineering in high school because I wasn't sure if I wanted to do hardware or software... but I knew I wanted to do one. I'd tried my hand at teaching myself earlier on, but I was trying to learn C++ without much guidance and it just didn't go well. Early on in college I took an intro to C course which I absolutely loved and programming has been an pretty intrinsic part of my life every sense.

### Have you worked professionally as a programmer?

Yes. I started part time in college (around 2011ish?) at a company called ADTRAN. My experience there was pretty varied including embedded development, test automation, devops, and web dev. After I graduated in 2014 I worked for a DoD contractor building data visualization software and web apps for the army rotary fleet at red-stone arsenal in Huntsville. From there I transitioned into the role of frontend architect at Scripps Networks Interactive (now Discovery) helping guide the web technical strategy for HGTV, Food Network, and Travel Channel. For the last few years I've been at Artsy where I've helped work on frontend performance, building out a design system, optimizing our CI deployments, spinning up eCommerce functionality, and being a tech lead for our ecommerce focused team. Most of that latter work is open source so you can check a lot of it out on GitHub.
